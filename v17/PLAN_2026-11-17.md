# PLAN - paper 02 rework (v17): pre-specification of every test

Written 2026-11-17 about 20:14 IST (file last saved 20:15 after a typo fix; the first evaluative run, a BTC pipeline test, started later), BEFORE any strategy-versus-random result was computed on the new data.
Nothing below is tuned; nothing is searched over. Every test listed here will be run and every result reported, including nulls and results that weaken the v16 claims. Anything added later goes in the section "Amendments" with its own timestamp and is labelled post hoc in the paper.

## P0. The rule is frozen
- Engine: `code/msnr_frozen.py`, a byte-for-byte copy of `D:\Malaysian-SNR\backtest\msnr.py` (sha256 543bb8c74b52e07c3928ea756ff70a4bc6c28b8a40a88f44bd677a944159891e; identical to the phase-2/3 copy in followup/lookahead/code_copy).
- Variants V0 (literal), V1 (obvious levels), V2 (storyline), V3 (Quasimodo), V4 (higher-timeframe levels), exactly as in `run_all.py`: ATR length 14, stop buffer 0.1 ATR, fixed 2R target, one position at a time, stop checked before target, entry at the next bar's open, trade timeframe 1h/4h/1d with higher timeframe 4h/1d/1w.
- NO parameter grid (v16's 8-point grid is not re-run), no tuning, no 1R/3R/next-level variants, no limit entries, no filters. The canonical top-down (phase-3) method is NOT re-run in this rework (it needs 5-minute data for the metals that cannot be re-downloaded); its v16 numbers are carried over and labelled as the earlier data vintage.

## P1. Data and universe (fixed now)
- Crypto, Binance Vision spot 1h klines (data.binance.vision), all history to 2026-11-16: legacy assets BTC, ETH, SOL, PAXG (PAXG = gold token) and ten NEW assets chosen by the author before any run, all large caps with at least five years on Binance: BNB, XRP, ADA, DOGE, LTC, LINK, TRX, DOT, AVAX, BCH (USDT pairs). The list is a fixed convenience list of large, long-lived coins; it is survivor-biased (coins that died are absent). Survivor bias does not enter the edge-versus-random comparison (both sides trade the same series) but would flatter any buy-and-hold comparison.
- Metals, Dukascopy public datafeed 1h BID and ASK candles from 2008-01: XAUUSD, XAGUSD. Simulated on the mid price (average of bid and ask OHLC); the observed bid-ask spread is used for costs. This replaces the bid-only histdata and the 2.4-year Yahoo futures series for the phase-1 style test.
- FX, Dukascopy 1h BID/ASK from 2010-01: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD (MSNR is taught mainly to forex and gold traders; the source notes say so). Same mid/spread treatment.
- Bars: crypto UTC; metals and FX use the trading day starting 22:00 UTC (same shift as v16).
- If a Dukascopy download cannot be completed, that asset class is dropped from the new analysis, the paper says so, and v16 metals numbers are kept as the earlier vintage. No substitute data is silently used.
- Legacy-reproduction check: the frozen engine on the stored v16 parquet files must reproduce v16 phase-1 trade counts for BTC/ETH/SOL; the Binance Vision series are compared bar by bar with the stored API series on the overlap.
- Cells: asset x timeframe (1h, 4h, 1d) x variant (V0-V4). Per-cell tests only where a cell has at least 30 trades in the window tested.

## P2. Windows
- IN-SAMPLE (IS) = first 60% of bars after the ATR warm-up, OUT-OF-SAMPLE (OOS) = last 40%, per cell, as in v16. Because the rule has no fitted parameters, IS and OOS are both honest samples; the split is kept for comparability and for the persistence test (P8).
- FULL = IS + OOS.
- Confirmatory sample (never touched by the v16 study): the ten new coins and four FX pairs, FULL window. Re-test sample: BTC, ETH, SOL, PAXG, XAU, XAG with new data (longer, better-quality metals).

## P3. Random-entry benchmarks (same stop and target geometry, same costs, same window; 500 repetitions per cell and window; seeds fixed by cell index)
- B0 = v16 benchmark: direction by coin flip (V0, V1) or the storyline direction (V2-V4); entry bar uniform in the window.
- B1 (PRIMARY) = direction-matched: P(long) = the strategy's own long share in that cell and window (V0, V1); storyline direction for V2-V4 as in B0. Entry bar uniform.
- B2 = B1 plus volatility-state matched: each random trade's entry bar is drawn uniformly from the bars whose ATR/close lies in the same quintile (quintiles of the window's own ATR/close distribution) as a strategy trade drawn at random. Quintile edges use the whole window, which is a descriptive matching device, not a trading signal.
- The statistic: edge = strategy mean gross R minus mean of the random-repetition means. p (two-sided) = 2 x min(share of reps >= strategy, share of reps <= strategy), +1 corrected, capped at 1. z = edge / sd of the random-rep means.
- Known non-matching (unchanged from v16, to be stated): random trades may overlap; the strategy holds one position at a time; signal timing clusters in time.

## P4. Pooled inference and multiple testing
- Pooled edge for a group = trade-count-weighted mean of (strategy gross R minus the cell's random mean). Inference by MONTH-CLUSTER bootstrap (5,000 resamples; clusters = calendar months of the signal bar, resampled with all assets and cells in the group moving together, which handles cross-asset correlation in crypto). The CI and a two-sided p come from the bootstrap distribution. The random means are treated as fixed (their own simulation error is small and is reported).
- Groups: each variant x timeframe (15), each variant (5), each asset class (crypto, metals, FX) x variant, legacy vs confirmatory sample, all trades.
- Family F1: all per-cell two-sided gross-edge tests (cells with >= 30 trades, FULL window, benchmark B1): report the number with p < 0.05 against 5% expected, and Holm, Benjamini-Hochberg and Benjamini-Yekutieli adjusted counts at 5%.
- Family F2: the 15 variant x timeframe pooled tests: Holm-adjusted.
- Net-R question ("does anything survive costs"): one-sided H0: mean net R <= 0 per cell (trade-level t) and pooled by month-cluster bootstrap; BH over cells. Number of effective trials in the whole study = the number of cells (no parameter search).
- Also report the permutation-style view: histogram of per-cell p-values against uniform (a mixture of real winners would show a spike near 0).

## P5. Costs
- Crypto: per side 7 bps (v16 base case), PAXG 10 bps; carry 2% a year pro rata. Sensitivity grid (re-computed from stored trades, not re-simulated): 1, 2, 4, 7, 10 bps per side.
- Metals and FX: base case = causal rolling-median spread (median of bid-ask spread at 1h candle open over the previous 720 hourly bars), paid once per round trip (half at entry, half at exit) in price units, carry 3% (metals), 0% (FX). Sensitivity: spread x 0, 0.5, 1, 2; and the spread observed at the entry bar's own open. Metals commissions are zero in spread-only retail accounts; not added.
- Break-even cost: the cost per side (bps) at which pooled mean net R is zero, per group.

## P6. Power
- For each pooled group: SE from the month-cluster bootstrap; minimum detectable edge at 80% power, 5% two-sided = 2.80 x SE; for single cells use the trade-independent SE and the random-rep sd. Report in R per trade, in win-rate percentage points at a 2R target (edge = 3 x win-rate shift), and relative to the base-case cost per trade. State what edge sizes could and could not have been detected.
- Power curve: probability of rejection as a function of true edge (normal approximation with the cluster SE).

## P7. Regimes and sub-periods (benchmark restricted to the same regime: the random entry bars are drawn only from bars in that regime)
- Calendar year (strategy signal year).
- Trend regime at signal: sign of the trailing 90-day return (causal, known at the signal bar).
- Volatility regime: ATR/close at the signal bar above or at-or-below its trailing 365-day median (causal).
- Variants V0-V4 pooled over assets, B1. Family F3 = all regime-bucket x variant tests: Holm and BH.

## P8. Persistence
- Spearman rank correlation of IS edge and OOS edge across cells with >= 30 trades in both halves; share of cells with the same sign (as in v16).

## P9. Look-ahead
- Truncation test (as `code/06_truncation_lookahead_test.py` of the pack) on new-data series for one crypto and one metals series with the frozen engine. Any mismatch is reported.

## P10. Reporting rules
- All cell-level results go in an online-appendix table. Cells with < 30 trades are listed and counted. Results that conflict with v16 are reported as such. The title/abstract claims follow the evidence; if the new sample shows a significant gross edge anywhere, the paper says so.
